How entry works

From application to puck drop

  1. Apply

    The team manager submits the form below. You'll get an email confirming we received it.

  2. Review

    The organizing committee reviews applications. Acceptance emails go out by October 25.

  3. Acceptance

    If your team is accepted, you'll get an email with a private link to submit your roster and payment details.

  4. 48 hours

    Pay the $1,800 fee and submit your roster within 48 hours of acceptance to lock in your spot.
